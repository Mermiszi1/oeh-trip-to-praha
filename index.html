Your guidesPrague Pulse Tours is a licensed local walking-tour company with over 2,500 five-star Google reviews. Our group of 44 gets two guides per tour, so you'll actually hear them.

Ticking since 1410The Astronomical Clock on Old Town Square was first installed in 1410 and is still running, which makes it one of the oldest working astronomical clocks in the world.
?!
A legend in the atticThe Old-New Synagogue has been in use since the 13th century. Legend says the Golem, a clay giant from Prague folklore, still rests in its attic.
?!
Stacked in layersSpace in the Old Jewish Cemetery was so limited that graves were layered on top of each other, which is why the tombstones crowd together at odd angles.
?!
Go big or go homePrague Castle is often called the largest ancient castle complex in the world. Bring comfy shoes, there's a lot of it.
?!
Built very slowlySt. Vitus Cathedral inside the castle was started in 1344 and only finished in 1929, almost 600 years later.
That's just the trailer. Everything else, the stories, the legends and the gossip, you'll hear from the guides on the day. So please pay attention… there might be a quiz on the bus home.

WEATHER

Autumn in Praha

October in Prague is mild during the day and chilly in the morning and evening, with a good chance of a shower. Think layers, not one heavy outfit. We'll post the real forecast in the WhatsApp group a few days before we leave.

Showers on about 1 in 3 days. Bring an umbrella or rain jacket.

What should I bring?

You'll be happiest with one warm layer more than you think you need. The guided tour is about 4 hours outside.

CLOTHES

  • A proper warm jacket (puffer or winter coat)
  • Thermal or long-sleeve base layer
  • A thick jumper or hoodie
  • Scarf, beanie and gloves for the morning and evening
  • Warm socks, plus a spare pair in case your feet get wet
  • Waterproof, comfy shoes
  • Hoodie or small blanket for the early bus ride

You can travel light, but don't skip the jacket: evenings still drop to single digits.

CLOTHES

EVERYONE

Get the PID Lítačka app

The official Prague transport app: buy tickets, plan routes with live departures and see closures. Pay with your card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Download it before we leave so you're ready on arrival.

IPHONE · IPADApp Store ANDROIDGoogle Play

Buy in the app

Download PID Lítačka, pay by card and activate the ticket before you get on. It becomes valid 1 minute after you activate it. This is the cheapest option.

Or tap your card

Every tram and bus has a contactless ticket machine, usually by the middle door. Tickets bought on board are valid straight away. Card payment on board isn't guaranteed, so have the app as backup.

Or a paper ticket

Yellow machines at metro stations and some stops. Stamp it in the yellow validator before the metro escalators, or right after boarding a tram or bus.

Avoid a fine

  • There are no barriers, but inspectors in plain clothes check tickets often. No valid ticket means an on-the-spot fine.
  • An unstamped paper ticket counts as no ticket.
  • Never stamp a ticket you bought on board or in the app: that makes it invalid.
  • Keep your phone charged: your app ticket is your proof.

Money tips

  • Card payment works almost everywhere: cafés, shops, museums and transport.
  • If a card terminal or ATM offers to charge you in euro, choose CZK. Converting to euro on the spot usually costs you more.
  • Bring a little cash in CZK for small stalls. At the hotel you pay in crowns, not euro. Use ATMs inside banks and skip street exchange booths in the tourist centre.
